# Preparation plan adopts the wedding editorial system — 2026-09-10

## Scope

Only `/articles/plan-podgotovki-k-svadbe/` and its UX/history evidence changed.
Homepage, Direct, Telegram/MAX, EventBudjet, the article copy, schema, canonical
URL and the shared final conversion contour were preserved.

## Product decision

Wedding expert pages are one public Timur Gromov brand, not separate visual
products. `/scenario/` is the canonical source for the portrait-led Hero,
palette and editorial type roles. The preparation guide remains a structured
long-form plan rather than a scenario clone.

## Implementation

- Replaced the text-only peach Hero with the existing black-and-white Timur
  portrait, dark overlays, author attribution and metadata pattern.
- Rebuilt the guide's introductory, start, contents, stage and checklist rhythm
  around the Scenario editorial grid while retaining all twelve stage IDs.
- Kept the existing shared final CTA/popup renderer and its attribution values.
